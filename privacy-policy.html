Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Overview

TradeCanvas ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application that provides live candlestick charts, technical indicators, and drawing tools for Binance spot markets. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.

3. Local Data Storage

All user data — including chart drawings, indicator configurations, watchlists, custom price markers, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using SQLite and SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

4. Network Connections

The app connects to Binance's public API endpoints to retrieve real-time market data (prices, candlestick data, and ticker information). These connections are made directly to:

  • api.binance.com — REST API for market data
  • stream.binance.com — WebSocket streams for real-time prices

We do not operate any intermediary servers. No personal data is sent in these requests.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — Required to fetch live market data from Binance.
  • Notifications — Optional, used for price alert notifications.
  • Wake Lock — Keeps WebSocket connections alive for real-time data.

No permission is used to access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Fonts for typography, which may make network requests to Google's font servers. Google's privacy policy applies to those requests: Google Privacy Policy .

No other third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks are integrated.

7. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally and we do not operate backend servers, your data is protected by your device's built-in security measures (lock screen, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date.

8. Children's Privacy

TradeCanvas is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app displays financial market data for informational and educational purposes only.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
